About us

Good food.
Good company.

The Finch has been part of Smith Street since 2019. We run a short, focused menu that changes with the seasons — built around what's best right now, not what's always on. The wine list leans natural and small producer, and our team will point you somewhere interesting without making it a lecture.

We take walk-ins when we can. Bookings open three weeks ahead.
