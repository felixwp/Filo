The bar

A neighbourhood bar
on the beach.

Bar Elvina sits a two-minute walk from the sand. We opened here because Avalon deserved a proper bar — somewhere with a considered wine list, food that earns its place, and no pretension whatsoever.

The wine skews natural and low-intervention. The food is small plates — made to share, built around what comes in that week. Come for a glass. Stay for the evening.

"We wanted somewhere we'd actually want to go — so we built it."
